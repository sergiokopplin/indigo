About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Applied Mathematics and Computational Science at the University of Pennsylvania, advised by Bingxin Zhao and Weijie Su. I received my M.S. in Pure Mathematics from the University of Pennsylvania and my B.S. in Pure Mathematics & Applied Mathematics from the University of Toronto.

My research interests primarily lie in data privacy, with a focus on its applications to machine learning and the social sciences. I am also interested in high-dimensional statistics and its applications in biomedicine. In addition, I have a strong interest in improving the quality of peer review at AI/ML conferences, and our work will be incorporated into the ICML 2026 review process.
