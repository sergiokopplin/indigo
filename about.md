<h3 style="text-align:center;">Susheel Varma, PhD MBA FBCS</h3>

### Short Bio

Susheel Varma, PhD MBA FBCS, is Chief Data Officer at [Sage Bionetworks](https://sagebionetworks.org), where he leads a team of 55+ professionals managing a $40M+ annual portfolio of biomedical data infrastructure across cancer, neurodegeneration, rare disease, and digital health programmes. He co-leads the [Global Alliance for Genomics and Health (GA4GH)](https://www.ga4gh.org/) AI Work Stream, serves on the [UKRI Infrastructure Advisory Committee](https://www.ukri.org/who-we-are/governance-and-committees/infrastructure-advisory-committee/), and sits on the Scientific and Industry Board of the [Genomic Data Infrastructure (GDI)](https://gdi.onemilliongenomes.eu/) project. Previously CTO of [Health Data Research UK](https://www.hdruk.ac.uk/) and founder of the AI and Data Science team at the [UK Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk), his career spans academia, government regulation, and non-profit research across continents, with a consistent focus on open science, [FAIR data principles](https://www.go-fair.org/fair-principles/), and international standards for responsible data sharing.

### Long Bio

Susheel Varma, PhD MBA FBCS, is Chief Data Officer at [Sage Bionetworks](https://sagebionetworks.org), a non-profit biomedical research organisation based in Seattle. He leads the Science and Data team of 55+ professionals, overseeing a $40M+ annual portfolio spanning 50+ concurrent initiatives funded by the [National Institutes of Health](https://www.nih.gov/) (NIA, NCI, NINDS, NCATS, [ARPA-H](https://arpa-h.gov/)), foundations ([Gates Foundation](https://www.gatesfoundation.org/), [AACR](https://www.aacr.org/), [Children's Tumor Foundation](https://www.ctf.org/)), and commercial partners. His team builds and operates multi-petabyte data infrastructure supporting cancer genomics ([HTAN](https://humantumoratlas.org/), [AACR GENIE](https://www.aacr.org/professionals/research/aacr-project-genie/), [MC2](https://mc2center.org/)), Alzheimer's and exceptional longevity research ([AMP-AD](https://adknowledgeportal.synapse.org/)), rare disease programmes ([AMP-ALS](https://www.amp-als.org/)), and digital health initiatives.

An advocate for open science and international standards, Susheel co-leads the [Global Alliance for Genomics and Health (GA4GH)](https://www.ga4gh.org/) AI Work Stream, serves on the [UKRI Infrastructure Advisory Committee](https://www.ukri.org/who-we-are/governance-and-committees/infrastructure-advisory-committee/), and sits on the Scientific and Industry Board of the [Genomic Data Infrastructure (GDI)](https://gdi.onemilliongenomes.eu/) project and the [MONARCH Initiative](https://monarchinitiative.org/). He has contributed to GA4GH standards including [Beacon](https://beacon-project.io/), the [Tool Registry Service (TRS)](https://ga4gh.github.io/tool-registry-service-schemas/), [Workflow Execution Service (WES)](https://ga4gh.github.io/workflow-execution-service-schemas/), and [Data Repository Service (DRS)](https://ga4gh.github.io/data-repository-service-schemas/), and championed [FAIR data](https://www.go-fair.org/fair-principles/) frameworks now adopted across genomics and health research internationally.

Previously, Susheel founded and led the AI and Data Science Technology Policy team at the [UK Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk), where he co-authored the [Guidance on AI and Data Protection](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/artificial-intelligence/guidance-on-ai-and-data-protection/) and the [AI Risk Assessment Toolkit](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/artificial-intelligence/guidance-on-ai-and-data-protection/ai-and-data-protection-risk-toolkit/), influencing regulatory practice across public and private sectors. He provided strategic guidance on high-profile programmes including [NHS England's Federated Data Platform](https://digital.nhs.uk/services/federated-data-platform), [Our Future Health](https://ourfuturehealth.org.uk/), and [Great Ormond Street Hospital's DRIVE](https://www.gosh.nhs.uk/our-research/our-research-infrastructure/drive-unit/) initiative.

As Chief Technology Officer at [Health Data Research UK](https://www.hdruk.ac.uk/), he managed a £69M+ infrastructure portfolio and built teams of 200+ specialists. He established the national [Trusted Research Environments (TRE)](https://www.hdruk.ac.uk/access-to-health-data/trusted-research-environments/) network, creating federated infrastructure connecting 200+ data custodians serving 45,000+ researchers, and led the [Health Data Research Innovation Gateway](https://www.healthdatagateway.org/) and the [DARE UK](https://dareuk.org.uk/) programme. His [TRE principles and best practices](https://zenodo.org/record/5767586) paper remains a foundational reference for the international health data research strategy.

At [EMBL-European Bioinformatics Institute](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/), Susheel managed the [EOSC-Life](https://www.eosc-life.eu/) and [EOSC-Hub](https://www.eosc-hub.eu/) European research infrastructure programmes across 50+ partner institutions, and coordinated [ELIXIR's](https://elixir-europe.org/) Human Data Communities. Earlier, at the [University of Sheffield](https://www.sheffield.ac.uk/), he served as CTO and Translational Technology Officer, delivering cloud platforms for computational biomedicine and coordinating 25+ research projects with combined budgets exceeding €30M.

Susheel holds a PhD in Computational Systems Biology from the [University of Sheffield](https://www.sheffield.ac.uk/), an Executive MBA in Management of Research Infrastructures from the [University of Milan-Bicocca](https://en.unimib.it/), a Master of Science in Advanced Software Engineering from the [University of Sheffield](https://www.sheffield.ac.uk/), and a Bachelor of Technology from [Pondicherry University](https://www.pondiuni.edu.in/), India. He is a Fellow of the [British Computer Society](https://www.bcs.org/), a Lifetime Fellow of the [Software Sustainability Institute](https://www.software.ac.uk/), and a Member of the [Association for Computing Machinery](https://www.acm.org/). He has delivered keynote addresses at joint [European Commission](https://commission.europa.eu/) and [WHO](https://www.who.int/) sessions on FAIR data for pandemic preparedness, as well as at [International Data Week](https://internationaldataweek.org/). His publications appear in [Cell Genomics](https://www.cell.com/cell-genomics/home), [Nature Reviews Genetics](https://www.nature.com/nrg/), [Nature Biotechnology](https://www.nature.com/nbt/), [NeurIPS](https://neurips.cc/), the [BMJ](https://www.bmj.com/), and [JACC](https://www.jacc.org/), among others.
